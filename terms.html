SleepMix Terms of Use

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Provider: Johnson, K.K. (株式会社ジョンソン)

1. The service

SleepMix lets you play and mix relaxing ambient sounds. Free features include a selection of sounds and scenes, the mixer, the sleep timer and up to 3 saved mixes. Pro unlocks all current sounds and scenes, unlimited saved mixes and listening insights, and removes ads.

2. Subscriptions and the one-time pass

  • Monthly Pro and Annual Pro are auto-renewing subscriptions billed by Google Play to your Google account at the price shown at checkout, until you cancel.
  • Where offered, a free trial applies only if Google Play confirms that you are eligible. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions to avoid being charged.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro remains available until the end of the paid period. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies.
  • The One-time Pass is a single payment that unlocks the Pro sounds and features available at the time of purchase, for as long as the app is offered. Future add-on sound packs may be sold separately.
  • To use Pro on a new device, sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

3. Use

Do not copy, extract or redistribute the sounds outside the app. Use a reasonable volume, and do not use the app where you need to hear alarms or traffic.

SleepMix is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

The app is provided "as is". To the extent permitted by law, Johnson, K.K. is not liable for indirect or consequential damages. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under applicable consumer law.
